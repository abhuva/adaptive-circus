# Working agreement for this research project

The aim is a method an educator can use and question. Discussion can stay in conversation. When Marc asks to document something, reconcile it with the existing work.

- Start at the README for the current method. Cases belong in `cases.md`, source findings and their limits in `evidence.md`, and questions or proposed investigations in `inquiry.md`. The research directory holds the collection, its review, and worked applications. Treat source transcriptions as source material.
- Find earlier statements of the idea, including synonyms, examples and linked pages. Decide whether the new material confirms, narrows, challenges or replaces them. Edit the passage that owns the claim. Revise affected summaries or point to that passage. Git history preserves superseded wording.
- Keep a practitioner’s observation, a participant’s account, a published finding and an AI-proposed explanation distinguishable. Preserve genuine disagreement with a short account of what could distinguish the alternatives. A useful hypothesis can stay open.
- During exploration, keep the live question and next decision together in the inquiry. When a question closes, replace it with the outcome or link to where the result belongs. Revisit a claim when a counterexample, new source, changed purpose or attempted application bears on it.
- Close a documentation update with what changed in our understanding, which earlier claim was checked or revised, and what remains open. Aim for one useful next discussion rather than a growing list of speculative work.
