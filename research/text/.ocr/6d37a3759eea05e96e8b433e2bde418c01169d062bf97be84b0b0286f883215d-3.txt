CROSS-OVER
Cross-over refers to the category of patterns With naturally occurring
crossing actions built-in to notation. Indicating patterns as cross-over
does not effect the notation; however, it is useful to be aware and is
notated by asterisk mark following notation.
Cross-Over Example: 21 3*

Revere notations invert the standard order of actions within a pattern.
Reverse action is indicated using the letter x and can is applied in one
of two ways: to the pattern as whole or individually to specific notes
within the pattern. In the first case of reversing the effect of an entire
pattern the x symbol appears before notation and followed by a period
to indicate its overall effect.
