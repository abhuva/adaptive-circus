The Open position is the state of objects that change location or lanes
as part of their event. This condition is marked by the enclosure of
square brackets along With a dash mark, used to indicate the
transitioning state from one location to another. For example, the
Fixed-State pattern of 2345 can be transposed into an Open-State
variation by grouping the actions of each hand and assigning them a
relationship of transference. Writing as an Open-State pattern it would
be The enclosed bracket of [2—4] indicates that the object
in the 2 position is transferred to the 4 position before performing the
actioning event, which in this case is to roll the ball. The second set of
enclosed brackets mirrors the first set, but in this case it is performed
With the opposite hand on the other side of the board.
