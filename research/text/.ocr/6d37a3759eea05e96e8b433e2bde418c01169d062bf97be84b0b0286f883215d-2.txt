START POSITION
Start order is determined by whichever
Side the first notation is located and
favours proximity to the same side of
the body. This means that patterns
should begin from the side of the body
that is closest and does not cross-over
to reach the notation. This avoids
adding unintended complexity to
simpler activity; however, there are
circumstance where crossing-over is
intended and notated separately.

Note that for Forward Arrangement patterns the start position is determined
by the first notation within the pattern. However, in Lateral Arrangement

direction from this point.
