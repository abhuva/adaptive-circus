Transition Note: Clear communication is required in explaining the intention of Mixed-
State Activities. lt is possible to suggest intention of activities without the use of words,
but it is inconsistently successful as large portion of understanding is pre context based.
These notations are Asymmetric and represent the inverse expression of the other. lt is
not always constant that the Synchronous Tempo is more accessible for participants than
an Asynchronous Tempo. For this reason, trial and error experimentation between the two
different states of tempo in order to discover which tempo state is most naturally received
by an individual participant.
An Asynchronous Asymmetric set of the previous two notations. Sometimes easier for
participants than Synchronous Variation; however, it is good to challenge switching back
and forth between Tempos. Notice the quality of change over in transitioning between
tempos and work to reduce delays and improve quality of movement.
