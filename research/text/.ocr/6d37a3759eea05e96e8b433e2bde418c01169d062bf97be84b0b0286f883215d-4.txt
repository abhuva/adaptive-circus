In other situations such as With synchronous activity, the reversing of orders
must be indicated more clearly for each beat of notation. In this case
individual multiple x symbols are used through notation to indicate each
specific action of change. Beginning from the standard assumption of none
crossing action, any notation of x instructs the crossing or uncrossing of
sides. Synchronous crossing action causes sides to overlap in a way that
one side goes over and the other side goes under the other.
Note that alternating top and bottom crossing Sides has great impact on
changing the level of challenge. For this reason the notation assumes the order
of the first note in a synchronous set to be on the top-side. In the case of (x23)
the side that reaches for the 2 is ordered on top, whereas (x32) directs the
side that reaches for the 3 to be in the top order.
