Learning to juggle parallels the challenge of learning how to ride a bicycle or how to swim. It is not that the challenge of learning to juggle is too difficult, but that we never created the appropriate learning tools to make it accessible.

Training wheels enable person to set smaller challenges and goals and develop general balance capacities as they experience the activity, itself.

Floatation devices enable person to be in the water without drowning and develop their capacity for swimming while engaged in the activity, itself.

5-STEP JUGGLING Classical Model

Functional Model

(Step-1) Concepts Before Components Table & body juggle activities to develop comprehension and experience of task.

(Step-2) Sequencing Capacity: Cow Jumps Over the Moon Sequence to develop bi-lateral sequencing capacity necessary for the timing of 3-ball cascade.

(Steps-3,4,&5) Classical Continuation: Quick practice with one ball throws, then two balls, and finally 3balls. Many effective variations of the Classical Model exist and tutors are encouraged to use their preferred model of linear learning following the introduction of first two steps.
